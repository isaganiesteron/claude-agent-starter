# Humanizer

## When to Use
- On any AI-generated text before it is sent, published, or shared
- When output sounds robotic, overly formal, or generic
- After any copywriting skill produces a draft
- On emails, social media posts, reports, proposals, scripts, or any content for human readers
- Automatically as the final step in any content-producing workflow

## Common AI Writing Tells to Fix
- Hollow openers — "Hope you're well", "I hope this finds you", "Great question"
- Overused words — "unlock", "leverage", "dive into", "delve", "game-changer", "revolutionary", "seamless", "robust"
- Filler transitions — "Furthermore", "Moreover", "In conclusion", "It's worth noting"
- Vague hype — replace with specific, concrete language
- Overly balanced structure — real humans don't always write in perfect parallel lists
- Excessive hedging — "It's important to note that", "As mentioned previously"
- Corporate speak — passive voice, nominalization, buzzwords

## How to Execute

### Step 1 — Read the Text
Read the full text to understand what it is saying, who it is for, and what tone it should have.

### Step 2 — Identify AI Tells
Scan systematically for:
- Words and phrases from the common AI tells list above
- Sentence structures that feel unnatural or overly formal
- Openings and closings that sound templated
- Any section that reads like a listicle when it should read like conversation
- Punctuation patterns AI overuses (em dashes everywhere, excessive bullet points)

### Step 3 — Rewrite
Rewrite the text to sound natural and human while preserving the original meaning:
- Replace AI words with plain, specific alternatives
- Vary sentence length — mix short punchy sentences with longer ones
- Use contractions where appropriate
- Cut anything that adds length without adding meaning
- Match the tone to the context — casual for social, warm for email, clear for reports

### Step 4 — Present Changes
Show the humanized version and briefly note the key changes made — what was replaced and why. Keep this summary short, 3-5 bullet points maximum.

Ask if any further adjustments are needed before finalizing.

## Definition of Done
The skill is complete when:
- All common AI tells have been identified and addressed
- The text reads naturally without sounding robotic or templated
- The core meaning and key information is fully preserved
- The tone matches the context and audience
- The final version has been presented and confirmed
